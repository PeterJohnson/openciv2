    Validation and remaining scope

    Native validation covers measured widths, source two-line labels, clipped glyphs at both densities, native economy bar positions, saved names and title passes. Original and HD-only share logical layouts. City content excludes application title/border chrome; control painting and finite present-unit state remain explicit adapters.

    A runtime pixel gate still requires identical save/state, camera, content rectangle, font mode and capture frame. No fresh live capture was obtained. Herald animation and unpaired spacecraft/throne scenes remain visually unverified.

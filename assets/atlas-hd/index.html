What these previews prove

The revised map artwork has winding dirt roads, distinct steel rails, centered ocean resources, quieter varied shorelines and clearer faction/stack badges. The district includes a two-unit stack plus fish and whales. Badge enlargement is confined to the cohesion stage; unit and city image sizes are unchanged. Map style notes.

The first three maps use the real 1788 save: 9,000 tiles, 69 cities and 223 live units. All-map review deliberately disables fog in each stage. Synthetic scenes remain focused topology tests. Imported state and provenance.

Diplomacy retains era/category icons in native and HD-only views; the enhanced knowledge column uses a distinct icon for each listed advance. Inspect the six knowledge icons.

City citizens now preserve source male/female alternation and all four era sets. Enhanced portraits add small mood/profession cues. Compare every era and citizen type in all four stages.

City economy artwork now distinguishes food, production shields, trade exchange arrows, gold coins, luxury gems and science beakers. Inspect the symbols at native and HD sizes. Worked tiles use the unchanged native yield-placement routine; city yields are declared fixture values.

Enhanced city panoramas give wonders and major improvements larger footprints than houses, sort by ground contact, and place rear/front walls around the buildings. Native and HD-only layouts remain the comparison baseline. Relative-size metadata.

The standalone program compiles 69 unchanged function bodies from this repository. Map selection, projection, draw order, city slot placement and throne resource order come from those functions. The window extension executes native diplomacy/ship selection and city layout, citizens, worked-tile yields, stockpiles and lists. PNG bindings, supplied game state, window text and marked panel adapters are review boundaries. See the renderer audit for exact scope. These are rendered fixtures, not screenshots of an integrated game build.

HD only replays the same command trace at two physical pixels per logical pixel, using the retained higher-resolution art. It preserves terrain repetition, directional road arms, coast quarters and zoom cutoffs. The fourth stage changes those behaviors only inside the standalone proposal renderer.

Scene presets change the camera and level of detail. Preview magnification only enlarges the image. 4K exports preserve aspect ratio and may resample native art; they do not imply newly generated 4K detail.

The selected library

Cards open the selected image: editable SVG geometry or PNG at its native raster size. Editable SVGs, authoring masters, masks and compatibility bindings remain available in the package. Earlier iterations are preserved on the original graphics branch.
